# Friction-loop numerical-work phase audit (56H2B)

Companion repository for the Lubricants Paper B study of numerical work estimated from sparsely retained force samples while recorded displacement remains available.

## Scope

The research compares force-reconstruction and numerical-work estimation across **finite, explicitly defined sampling phases**, with separate studies of the Imperial/PoliTO and EUCENTRE recorded friction-loop sources. The published study should not be interpreted as a field metrology certification or a guarantee for untested sampling schedules.

**Data distinction:** Original third-party experimental recordings are **not redistributed here**. Source-specific scripts and author-generated derived score ledgers may be supplied in the repository's release archive. Use the official provider records for independent raw-input reruns.

## Original measurements (obtain from providers)

- Imperial/PoliTO source: [Fantetti et al., Mendeley Data V1](https://doi.org/10.17632/gy587m7gx7.1).
- EUCENTRE/BED source: [BED Experiments V1](https://doi.org/10.60756/euc-ubsy26g318).
- Historical Hannover source: [LUIS dataset](https://doi.org/10.25835/66r5bbkq); previous Hannover-specific analytical repository: [friction-work-32samples](https://github.com/shaikhamalkawi-ux/friction-work-32samples).
- Separate ARTS source: [ARTS friction-damper dataset](https://github.com/ARTS-Laboratory/Dataset-Friction-Damper-with-Backlash).

## Reproduction interpretation

- Derived-ledger/identity checks and independent third-party raw-data replays are different verification levels.
- Source identity, hashes, finite sampling definitions, and claim boundaries must be preserved when comparing results.
- Recorded force/displacement errors, instrument calibration, physical dissipation, and source processing uncertainty are not interchangeable.
- Do not pool or re-label the Imperial and EUCENTRE study conditions as independent laboratories of one specimen.

## Main analysis numbers reported by the research package

The archived derived-verification register reports **48,600 Imperial phase-score rows**, **60,750 EUCENTRE phase-score rows**, **12,150 R5 paired rows**, and **48,600 R6 channel-recombination rows**. The archived analyses report **17/108** Imperial condition-budget method-choice changes under the enlarged phase set and **143/2025** R5 phase-winner changes. These are archived derived analyses, **not** new independent raw-data reruns by this repository upload.
